How the numbers work

Every number on this site comes from public records and links to the document behind it. The data and the code that produce it are public.

Bills, rules, and orders

Every state and federal bill introduced since January 2025, plus federal rules and executive orders, that is substantially about artificial intelligence, algorithms, automated decisions, synthetic media, or AI data centers.

Fears and controls

Each measure is read for the fears it cites and the controls it would impose, using fixed definitions for each. A first pass proposes labels, and a second pass must back every label with a quote copied from the measure's own text, which is then checked word for word. Only labels that survive both count anywhere on this site.

Who's funding it

Lobbying reported on federal filings whose issue descriptions name one of the tracked fears, over the past four quarters. Amounts on a lobbying filing cover everything on that filing, so they measure spending by organizations that lobby on these fears, not spending on the fear alone. Filings that mention AI without naming a tracked fear are still shown on each organization's page and in the downloadable data.

Election money

Money raised and spent by political committees working on AI policy, counted by committee rather than by donor, and kept separate from the funding ranking because a committee's spending does not say which side of a fear it is on. Individual donations appear on the receiving committee's page, each linked to the filing it came from.

Who benefits

Government agencies and officials that a measure would give new authority, duties, or enforcement power over AI, counted by the number of measures.

Public attention

Daily Wikipedia pageviews for each fear's articles, and daily news volume from GDELT.
